## [OpenWrt One Open Hardware Router Released](https://openwrt.org/toh/openwrt/one) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The OpenWrt project and Software Freedom Conservancy have released the OpenWrt One, an open-source single-board router designed to run OpenWrt natively. It features a MediaTek Filogic 820 SoC, dual-band Wi-Fi 6, 1GB of RAM, and is priced at $89 without case and antennas. This release provides a fully open-source, hacker-friendly router that embodies the right to repair, offering a reliable alternative to commercial routers with limited support. It strengthens the open networking ecosystem and allows users to extend device lifespan with community-driven firmware. The OpenWrt One includes 1GB of DDR4 RAM, 256MB NAND flash, 16MB NOR flash for recovery, an M.2 SSD slot, a USB-C serial console, and dual Ethernet (2.5Gbit WAN and 1Gbit LAN). A version with WiFi 7, the 'OpenWrt Two', is already in development.

hackernews · peter_d_sherman · Jul 6, 18:23 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48808482)

**Background**: OpenWrt is a popular open-source firmware for routers and embedded devices, but it typically requires installation on third-party hardware. The OpenWrt One is the first official hardware platform co-developed by the OpenWrt project and Software Freedom Conservancy, aiming to provide a fully open reference design.

## [Anthropic discovers global workspace in LLMs](https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Anthropic researchers have identified a set of internal representations in their Claude model that function like a 'global workspace', a concept from neuroscience, which may improve reasoning and safety. This discovery could lead to better understanding of how language models process information, enhancing model interpretability and safety, and potentially inspiring new architectures. The global workspace was specifically observed in Claude Opus 4.5, where a small cluster of representations is shared across diverse contexts and influences final outputs.

hackernews · in-silico · Jul 6, 17:44 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48808002)

**Background**: Global workspace theory (GWT), proposed by Bernard Baars in 1988, is a cognitive architecture that explains conscious access as information being globally available to many processes. Anthropic's work is among the first to test for functional analogs of GWT in large language models, using experiments inspired by neuroscience.

## [Tencent Releases Hy3: 295B MoE Model under Apache 2.0](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/6/hy3/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent released Hy3, a 295B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model with 21B active parameters and 3.8B MTP layer parameters, under the Apache 2.0 license. It outperforms similar-size models and rivals flagship open-source models with 2-5x parameters. This release strengthens the open-source AI ecosystem by providing a highly efficient large model that reduces computational cost while maintaining high performance. It also signals Tencent's significant investment in AI and open collaboration. The model supports a context length of 256K tokens. The full model is 598GB on Hugging Face, with an FP8 quantized version at 300GB. It is available for free on OpenRouter until July 21st.

rss · Simon Willison · Jul 6, 23:57

**Background**: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) is a model architecture where only a subset of parameters (experts) are activated for each input, enabling large model capacity with lower inference cost. Multi-Token Prediction (MTP) layers further enhance efficiency by predicting multiple future tokens simultaneously. These techniques allow Hy3 to achieve strong performance with only 21B active parameters out of 295B total parameters.

## [Hacker Arrested via Microsoft's GDID Device ID](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/microsoft-device-telemetry-key-to-unmasking-alleged-scattered-spider-hacker-627148) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The FBI arrested 19-year-old Peter Stokes by using Microsoft's Global Device ID (GDID) to track his Windows device, despite his use of a VPN. This case reveals that Microsoft's GDID is a persistent device identifier that law enforcement can use to bypass VPN anonymity, raising significant privacy concerns for all Windows users. The GDID is generated during Windows installation and does not change with updates; only a clean reinstall creates a new identifier. Investigators cross-referenced GDID data with login records from Snapchat, Apple, and Facebook to confirm the suspect's identity.

However, operating systems and applications often include unique device identifiers that can be transmitted alongside traffic. Microsoft's GDID is one such identifier that persists across network changes, making it possible to tie online activity to a specific device even when IP addresses are masked.

## [SpaceX Falcon 9 Debris Creates Lithium Plume in Upper Atmosphere](https://t.me/zaihuapd/42387) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A study published in Communications Earth & Environment on February 19, 2026, reports that the uncontrolled re-entry of a SpaceX Falcon 9 upper stage over Europe in February 2025 produced a persistent lithium plume at 96-100 km altitude, with lithium concentration increasing by a factor of 10. This finding provides direct evidence that rocket debris from commercial space launches is measurably polluting Earth's upper atmosphere, which was previously considered largely pristine, raising environmental concerns for the rapidly growing space industry. Using ground-based lidar, German scientists detected the lithium plume about 20 hours after re-entry, measuring a tenfold increase in lithium atoms at an altitude of 96 km. The rocket's upper stage had suffered an oxygen leak, leading to uncontrolled re-entry and debris fall in Poland.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 6, 11:17

**Background**: Rocket bodies and satellites re-entering the atmosphere vaporize, releasing metals like lithium, aluminum, and copper. The upper atmosphere (mesosphere and lower thermosphere, ~80-120 km) receives around 50-80 grams of lithium daily from small meteors, but a single Falcon 9 rocket injected a comparable or larger amount in one event. Lidar (light detection and ranging) uses laser pulses to measure atmospheric composition at specific altitudes.
