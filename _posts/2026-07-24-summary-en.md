## [Astronomers may have found the first exomoon](https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2610/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Astronomers have identified a candidate exomoon, designated CD-35 2722 b I, orbiting a brown dwarf in the CD-35 2722 system. This marks the first potential detection of a moon outside our solar system. If confirmed, this would be the first exomoon ever discovered, opening a new frontier in exoplanetary science and challenging existing classification systems for celestial bodies. It also highlights the difficulty of detecting such small, distant objects. The candidate exomoon orbits a brown dwarf about 80 times the mass of Jupiter, which itself orbits a primary star in a binary system. The detection was made using transit timing variations and other indirect methods.

hackernews · MarcoDewey · Jul 23, 14:02 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49021783)

**Background**: An exomoon is a natural satellite that orbits an exoplanet or other extrasolar body. Brown dwarfs are substellar objects with masses between 13 and 80 Jupiter masses, too small to fuse hydrogen but capable of fusing deuterium. Detecting exomoons is extremely challenging because they are small and faint compared to their host bodies.

## [PyPI blocks file uploads to releases older than 14 days](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/23/seth-larson/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

PyPI now rejects new file uploads to releases older than 14 days, effective July 22, 2026. This restriction aims to prevent poisoning of long-stable releases in case publishing tokens or workflows are compromised. This update significantly improves supply chain security for the Python ecosystem by closing a vulnerability that could allow attackers to inject malicious code into trusted, stable releases. It protects millions of users who rely on PyPI for package distribution. The restriction was implemented via Warehouse PR #19727. As of the announcement, no known abuse of this attack vector had occurred, but the exploit was technically possible and had not been mitigated before.

rss · Simon Willison · Jul 23, 04:50

**Background**: PyPI (Python Package Index) is the official repository for Python packages, used by millions of developers. Recent supply chain attacks, such as the LiteLLM attack in March 2026 and the Hades attack in June 2026, demonstrated how compromised publishing tokens can lead to malicious packages being uploaded to trusted accounts. By blocking uploads to old releases after 14 days, PyPI reduces the window of opportunity for such attacks.

China has been aggressively promoting IPv6 adoption to increase its influence over internet infrastructure. China previously proposed a similar 'New IP' protocol at the International Telecommunication Union (ITU), but it faced opposition and did not gain approval.

This incident highlights a critical safety vulnerability in electric vehicles where reliance on electronic door mechanisms without mechanical backups can trap occupants after a collision, potentially influencing future safety standards and EV door design. The vehicle was traveling at 167 km/h at impact; the collision caused a short circuit in the high-voltage traction battery, which then led to a loss of power in the 12V low-voltage system that powers door actuators; the car lacked any external mechanical door handles.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 24, 00:56

**Background**: Modern electric vehicles often use electronic door release mechanisms controlled by the low-voltage (12V) system, which also powers critical control modules. In a severe crash, if the high-voltage battery is compromised, the low-voltage system can lose power, rendering electronic doors inoperable. Some cars include mechanical override handles for emergency exit, but not all do.
