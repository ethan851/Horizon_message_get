## [First Fruit of New Lychee-Longan Hybrid 'Huaishi' Achieved](https://news.ycwb.com/ikimvkmtkh/content_54155752.htm) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The first fruit of 'Huaishi', a new intergeneric hybrid between lychee (as maternal parent) and longan (as paternal parent), has been harvested for the first time. The hybrid was developed by a team from the Guangdong Academy of Agricultural Sciences starting in 2015, successfully breaking the reproductive barrier between the two genera. This breakthrough demonstrates that intergeneric hybridization in fruit trees is possible, potentially opening new avenues for breeding improved fruit varieties with desirable traits. The hybrid combines the flavors of both parents and shows early ripening, which could benefit growers and consumers. The hybrid's parents are the late-maturing lychee 'Huaizhi' and the longan 'Shixia', but the offspring exhibits early maturity. The fruit is described as crisp, sweet, and fragrant, with a clear advantage over early-maturing lychees that are typically sour.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 4, 12:31

**Background**: Intergeneric hybridization involves crossing plants from different genera, which is often hindered by reproductive barriers. In plants, such barriers can be pre-zygotic (e.g., differences in flowering time, pollen incompatibility) or post-zygotic (e.g., embryo abortion). The Guangdong team likely used techniques like embryo rescue to overcome these barriers.
