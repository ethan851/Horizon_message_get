## [Entire Herculaneum Scroll Read for First Time with AI](https://scrollprize.org/firstscroll) ⭐️ 9.0/10

The Vesuvius Challenge team has read the entire Herculaneum scroll PHerc. 1667 for the first time using AI and machine learning techniques, virtually unwrapping and detecting ink to reveal around 22 columns of Greek text. The achievement is documented in a preprint and open-source code released on GitHub. This breakthrough unlocks knowledge from nearly 2,000 years ago, offering unprecedented access to ancient texts that were thought lost forever. It demonstrates the transformative potential of AI in archaeology and paves the way for reading hundreds more carbonized scrolls. The scroll, sealed since the eruption of Mount Vesuvius in 79 AD, is approximately 1.4 meters long and was read using CT scans and machine learning to identify the ink. The entire scroll was virtually unwrapped and read end-to-end, confirming the viability of the technique.

hackernews · verditelabs · Jun 25, 15:48 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48675179)

**Background**: The Herculaneum scrolls were carbonized by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius, making them too fragile to unroll physically. Previous attempts often damaged the brittle papyrus. The Vesuvius Challenge, launched in 2023, incentivized teams to develop non-invasive methods using X-ray CT and AI. This is the first time an entire scroll has been read without physical unrolling.

## [IBM unveils first sub-1nm chip with 0.7nm transistors](https://www.reuters.com/business/ibm-unveils-tech-chip-smaller-than-1-nanometer-ai-computing-push-2026-06-25/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

IBM announced the world's first sub-1 nanometer chip technology featuring 0.7nm transistors and the novel NanoStack 3D stacking architecture, promising up to 50% performance improvement or 70% energy efficiency gain. This breakthrough extends Moore's Law beyond conventional scaling, enabling significantly higher transistor density and performance for AI and high-performance computing. It sets a new benchmark for the semiconductor industry and could influence future chip manufacturing for the next decade. The chip integrates nearly 100 billion transistors in an area the size of a fingernail, with double the density of IBM's 2021 2nm chip. Production is expected to start within five years, but manufacturing partners have not been announced yet.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 25, 15:39

**Background**: For decades, chip performance improved by shrinking transistor size, a trend known as Moore's Law. As physical limits approach, manufacturers are turning to 3D stacking, where transistors are built vertically rather than side by side. IBM's NanoStack architecture is a complementary FET (CFET) design that stacks n-type and p-type transistors on top of each other, achieving greater density and performance.

## [UN Adopts First Global Technical Regulation for Autonomous Driving](https://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202606/25/WS6a3cf7e7a310986e2b461ebc.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On June 25, 2026, the United Nations World Forum for Harmonization of Vehicle Regulations (WP.29) voted to adopt the first global technical regulation for automated driving systems (ADS GTR), co-led by China, the EU, the UK, the US, Canada, and Japan. This milestone establishes a common safety framework for autonomous driving systems worldwide, which is expected to accelerate industrialization and international trade in autonomous vehicles, while also promoting the alignment of national standards with global norms. The regulation covers product lifecycle management, including safety management, safety documentation, testing verification, and post-deployment safety, and it specifies core technical indicators. China's domestic mandatory standard, already finalized, is consistent with the ADS GTR, with additional details for L3 and L4 systems.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 25, 16:03

**Background**: WP.29 is a permanent working party under the UN Economic Commission for Europe, responsible for harmonizing vehicle regulations globally. Autonomous driving systems are vehicles that can operate with reduced or no human input. The ADS GTR is the first international regulation specifically for such systems, previously there was no unified global standard.

## [German Court Holds Google Liable for AI Overview Errors](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/25/ai-and-liability/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A German court ruled that Google is legally liable for false information generated by its AI Overviews, treating the AI's outputs as Google's own words. This landmark decision establishes that companies cannot evade responsibility by blaming faulty AI systems. The ruling sets a crucial precedent for AI liability, ensuring that companies deploying AI agents must take responsibility for their outputs. It prevents a regulatory loophole that would allow businesses to profit from cheaper AI while avoiding accountability for mistakes. The case specifically concerns Google's AI Overviews, which generate search result summaries using generative AI. Bruce Schneier commented that AI agents should be treated as agents of the deploying organization, just like human employees.

rss · Simon Willison · Jun 25, 22:28

**Background**: Google AI Overviews is an AI feature integrated into Google Search that produces AI-generated summaries of search results. It has faced criticism for inaccuracies and reducing website traffic. The legal question of who is liable when an AI agent makes a mistake has been debated globally, with this German ruling providing a clear answer: the deploying company is liable.
