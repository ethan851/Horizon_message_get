## [Chinese-led BESIII collaboration confirms existence of glueballs](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/pvyNR1lN7QPx3IrpB3WtUg) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On August 6, 2026, the BESIII collaboration, led by Chinese scientists at the Institute of High Energy Physics, announced that after 15 years of research it has confirmed the existence of glueballs. The team showed that the particle X(2370), first discovered in 2011, has quantum properties and decay patterns consistent with a glueball-dominated state. This is the first experimental confirmation of glueballs, a new form of matter predicted by the Standard Model but never before observed. The result is a major landmark for particle physics and offers a stringent test of quantum chromodynamics, the theory governing the strong force. The analysis is based on a sample of 10 billion J/ψ decays recorded by BESIII at the BEPC-II collider. In 2024, the collaboration determined the spin-parity of X(2370) to be 0⁻⁺, matching lattice QCD predictions, and has now identified multiple new decay modes that confirm its flavor-singlet quantum numbers.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 07:31

**Background**: Glueballs are composite particles made entirely of gluons, the force-carrying particles of the strong interaction. Unlike ordinary hadrons, such as protons and neutrons, they contain no valence quarks. The Standard Model predicts their existence, but they have been notoriously difficult to identify because they can mix with ordinary quark-antiquark mesons. BESIII, located at the Beijing Electron-Positron Collider, is a leading experiment for studying J/ψ decays, which are gluon-rich and therefore promising for producing glueballs.

On August 6, 2026, AMD announced a definitive agreement to acquire Taalas, an AI inference chip startup co-founded by former Tenstorrent CEO Ljubisa Bajic. AMD plans to integrate Taalas's model-in-silicon technology with its Instinct GPUs to deliver breakthrough inference performance and efficiency. This acquisition strengthens AMD's position in the rapidly growing AI inference market by adding a novel approach that hardwires model weights directly into silicon. It could intensify competition with Nvidia and specialized AI chip startups, while signaling a broader industry trend toward embedding models in hardware for efficiency gains. Taalas's current chip runs a small version of Meta's Llama 3.1, and the company is developing chips for larger and more advanced models. This is conceptually similar to Google's experimental work of fitting quantized models onto individual TPUs, as mentioned in the community discussion.

## [ByteDance Weighs Training 5-Trillion-Parameter LLM](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/_SGStRsaJmpos2_deXUs8A) ⭐️ 8.0/10

ByteDance is reportedly discussing training a large language model with over 5 trillion parameters, led by Xiang Liang of the Seed Foundation in collaboration with data lead Shen Ke. The plan, still in early stages, would surpass Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-Max and Moonshot's K3 to become the largest known model in China. This reveals ByteDance's ambition to compete at the frontier of AI model scale and signals a strategic pivot under Zhang Yiming's leadership, who reportedly rejects knowledge distillation for merely copying existing capabilities. If realized, it could reshape the domestic LLM race and push the boundaries of training infrastructure in China. At a Seed all-hands meeting two weeks ago, Zhang Yiming explicitly opposed the distillation approach, arguing it only replicates Claude's existing abilities and cannot achieve true breakthrough, and encouraged the team to pursue the ceiling of intelligence while accepting short-term setbacks. Seed is also reorganizing, dismantling the 'horse race' mechanism, and consolidating resources to push the project forward.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 6, 13:10

**Background**: ByteDance Seed is the AI research division of ByteDance, founded in early 2023, responsible for foundation models behind the Doubao chatbot and Seedance/Seedream generators. Knowledge distillation (KD) is a technique for transferring capabilities from large proprietary models like GPT-4 to smaller open-source models, often used to cut training cost, but it typically cannot produce models that exceed the teacher's abilities. The reported target of 5 trillion parameters would position ByteDance above Qwen 3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model from Alibaba released in August 2026.

## [OpenAI marks GPT-5 anniversary with Agent Plugins open standard](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/06/gpt-5-turning-one-as-openai-shares-new-agent-plugins-standard/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On the eve of GPT-5's first anniversary, OpenAI and partners (AWS, Cursor, GitHub, Microsoft, Vercel) published Agent Plugins, an open standard for packaging reusable AI agent skills and MCP server configurations. The standard allows a plugin built once to work across compatible agent clients. Agent Plugins could reduce fragmentation in the AI agent ecosystem by making agent extensions portable across competing products. It gives developers a vendor-neutral way to distribute skills, potentially strengthening open standards like MCP and the broader agent economy. The format packages Agent Skills (e.g., SKILL.md-based folders) and supports MCP server configurations in a shared format, and is under open governance with a steering committee including Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Vercel. The announcement coincides with GPT-5's anniversary; OpenAI hasn't officially announced GPT-6, and GPT-5.6's release was briefly delayed by a U.S. government security review.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 7, 00:46

**Background**: Agent Skills are a lightweight, open format for extending AI agents with specialized knowledge and workflows, typically a folder with a SKILL.md file. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard introduced by Anthropic in November 2024 that standardizes how AI apps connect to external tools and data, often described as a 'USB-C port for AI applications.' Agent Plugins builds on these concepts to make agent capabilities distributable and interoperable across clients from different vendors.
